apple music authorisation

step 1 — enter your developer token

Generate this in your plugin settings (it is derived from your Team ID, Key ID, and .p8 private key). You can also generate it manually and paste it below.

step 2 — authorise with apple

Click the button below to open Apple's sign-in dialog. After granting access your user music token will appear below.

step 3 — copy your user music token

Paste this value into the User Music Token field in your Apple Music plugin settings.

copied!
Note: User music tokens are valid for 6 months. After they expire you will need to re-authorise here and update the token in your plugin settings.
